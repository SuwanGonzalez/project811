🫧 Simple skin-care routine

Gentle and consistent beats using lots of products.

Morning

  1. Wash with a gentle cleanser, or rinse if your skin is dry.
  2. Use a fragrance-free moisturizer.
  3. Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen.

Night

  1. Gently remove sunscreen/makeup and cleanse.
  2. Moisturize; avoid scrubs and adding several new actives at once.
  3. Change pillowcase regularly and keep hands off irritated spots.
If your skin is suddenly much worse, painful, swollen, very itchy, or leaving scars, ask a healthcare professional or dermatologist. A simple routine is a good start, but it may not treat the cause.
